The Digital Metamorphosis

LunaLinks required a platform that didn't feel like a "store," but a digital boutique. We focused on high-concurrency handling using Node.js and a seamless frontend with React.

The biggest challenge was presenting microscopic details of the jewellery without compromising on mobile performance. We implemented a custom-tiled image rendering engine for 360-degree product views.
